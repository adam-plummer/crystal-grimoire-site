Testers

Crystal Grimoire · closed test, Android only

Thanks for helping! Crystal Grimoire is an idle game. A silence called the Hush has put out every light in the valley. You wake the seven crystals in an old tower, gather familiars who make Mana for you, even while you're away, and fill a Lantern that pushes the Hush back one night at a time.

Before a new developer can release a game, Google needs at least 12 people to test it for 14 days. That's what you're joining.

Getting it

  1. Join the testers' group. That's how Google Play knows you're a tester.
    Join the group
  2. Opt in to the test, using the same Google account.
    Opt in
  3. Install the game from the Google Play link on the opt-in page. It can take a few minutes after you opt in before Play lets you install it.

It's Android only for now, so iPhones can't join, sorry.

The one big ask: please keep the game installed and stay opted in for the full 14 days, even if you only play a few times. If too many people drop out, the 14 days start again.

What to do

Play however you like. If you can, play your first day without reading up on anything first. Seeing how a brand-new player gets on is exactly what I can't test myself.

A few minutes at a time is plenty. Open it, spend what your familiars have made, and put it down again. The first nights come quickly. After the fourth, the Lantern burns right through a night and most of the next day, so a new night comes about once a day, and nothing can hurry it.

When the game asks about Play data, please say Yes. It sends me anonymous numbers about each night, such as how far you've come and how long it took, which is how I see where people get stuck. It's never linked to your name, and you can turn it off in Settings at any time.

Good to know

  • Updates come through the Play Store like any app, so there's no need to uninstall. If you're signed in to Google Play Games, your whole game is saved to the cloud and comes back after a reinstall or on a new phone.
  • The ads are real. Because the game isn't in the shop yet, they may not always turn up; if the game says no ad came, that's why. Please only watch one when you want the gift. Watching or tapping ads to help me out can get my ad account closed.
  • You don't need to buy anything. Remove Ads would cost real money, so please don't.
  • The story runs for weeks, longer than the test. Near the end of the 14 days, if you'd like to see the later parts, Settings > Help has Jump to Act 3 and Jump to the Prism. They replace your own game and can't be undone, so only use them once you've played as far as you want to.

Feedback

Please fill in the short form twice: once after your first day, and once near the end of the two weeks. It takes about two minutes.

Give feedback

One honest line beats a polite paragraph. "I didn't know what the bar at the top wanted" is gold.

Questions or problems? Email crystalgrimoire.game@outlook.com, or use Settings > Contact support in the game. See the privacy policy for everything the game collects.
