Delete Your Account

Crystal Grimoire · Adam Plummer

You can delete your Crystal Grimoire account, and the data that goes with it, at any time. There are two ways.

In the game (straight away)

  1. Open the game and tap the cog at the right of the top bar to open Settings.
  2. Under Privacy and account, tap Delete my account.
  3. Hold Delete it until it fills, to confirm.

Your phone needs an internet connection. Everything listed below is deleted there and then, and the game starts again as a new install.

By email (if you no longer have the game)

Email crystalgrimoire.game@outlook.com with the subject Delete my account. If you signed in with Google Play Games, include your Play Games gamer name, so I can find your records. I will delete them and reply within one month.

What is deleted

  • the game's random player ID, and with it the link to your Google Play Games account;
  • the copy of your game saved to the cloud, if you signed in with Play Games;
  • your play data held by Unity Analytics, if you shared it;
  • in the game route, your game and its backups on that phone.

What is kept

  • Purchases. Remove Ads belongs to your Google account, and Google Play keeps its own record of it, so it comes back if you install the game again.
  • Crash reports. These carry no name or player ID and are deleted automatically after Unity's retention period.

For everything the game collects and why, see the privacy policy.
