Crystal Grimoire

An idle game of seven crystals, a tower and a moth called Wick.

Coming soon to Google Play.

Wake the seven crystals in Elowen's tower and bring the valley's lights back. Tap to make Mana, gather familiars who make it for you, and each night pour the day's light into the Lantern.

Questions? Email crystalgrimoire.game@outlook.com.

How it was made: The pictures were created with Grok, the story pictures with GPT Image, and the crystals and Wick were modelled in Tripo. The music was made with Suno, and the sounds come from the Sonniss and Versilian sample libraries.
