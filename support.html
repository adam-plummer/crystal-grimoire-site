Support

Crystal Grimoire

Found a bug, lost a save, or stuck on something? Email crystalgrimoire.game@outlook.com and I'll get back to you, usually within a few days.

Reporting a problem

The easiest way is from the game: Settings > Contact support opens your email app with the game's version, your night and your phone already written in. Otherwise, it helps to include:

  • what happened, and what you were doing just before;
  • your phone's make and model;
  • the game's version (at the foot of the game's Settings).

Purchases

Remove Ads belongs to your Google account. If it's missing after a reinstall or on a new phone, open Settings > Restore purchases in the game, signed in to Google Play with the account you bought it on.

Deleting your data

See Delete your account, and the privacy policy for everything the game collects.
